About Cirava

Cirava is a Base-native digital asset project built around transparancy, disciplined token managment, and long-term ecosystem development.

Cirava has a fixed maximum supply of 100,000,000 tokens, with no public mint function. The token allocation is structured across founder and team vesting, liquidity, project treasury, community and ecosystem development, strategic growth, and long term reserve.
